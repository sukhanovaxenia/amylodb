mining: identity and role rules v3b-v3e, evidence tier for aggregators

Rules added after the lookup-diff reviews 2-4 and the v3c/v3d re-validations
(docs/changes_from_source.md, sections 9.6-9.11). Re-validated corpus v3e:
1870 proteins = 1455 kept + 415 dropped; 579 aggregators (curated 67, text
227, model_only 285); 697 condensate candidates (component 479, regulator 218).

  * a species word written before the name selects that organism's entry
    ("chicken cystatin"); no entry of an unnamed organism for a family run
    match or in a human-material abstract; Roman member marks kept in the
    qualifier-free variant; completed member names go through the aliases;
    TrEMBL names read from submission names
  * compare_lookup.py --exclude skips proteins reviewed on earlier sheets
  * an unnamed organism is refused only when the name designates a human
    family that was rejected (unique names such as melittin or RepA are
    accepted again); a rejected human family opens the search in other
    organisms; member completion through "(ABBR)" and digit+Greek marks;
    aliases COL4A1, RBP4, FGA, GSK3B, PLAT, LRP1, SOD1 and the pattern
    "histone deacetylase N" -> HDACN
  * after a rejected human family, member-adding run matches are refused in
    every organism; UniProt requests retry six times (backoff to 60 s) and a
    single 400/404 once; letter-numeral member marks ("C-II"); aliases for
    VHL spellings, SERPINA3, RB1
  * any paper's aggregator vote makes an aggregator also with
    --role-thresholds (the thresholds choose only the modifier subtype);
    curated synonyms match with hyphens read as spaces; lesion names (PHF,
    tangles, Lewy bodies, aggresomes) are aggregation terms
  * aggregation_evidence for kept aggregators: curated | text (a sentence
    states self-aggregation) | model_only; stored, exported, reported and
    shown on review sheets; condensate candidates from regulator votes carry
    the subtype "regulator"; alias ch-TOG -> CKAP5
  * the tier reaches the build: AmyloidRecord.aggregation_evidence, last
    column of records.tsv, graph nodes, reconcile_stats; strongest tier wins
    on merge; model_only (predicted) records vote with half weight;
    condensate_candidates.tsv gets condensate_role (component | regulator)

Tests: 285 passed.
